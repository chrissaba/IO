Changes: the name, icons and tab group title say IO, the group colour starts at purple, and a few
messages are reworded. The extension key (and so its id) is unchanged so Playwright MCP can reach it.

To install: chrome://extensions > turn on Developer mode > Load unpacked > choose this folder.
Remove the Web Store 'Playwright Extension' first; they share an id.
